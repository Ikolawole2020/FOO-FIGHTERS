Our Mission

Preserving Music Culture

The Foo Fighters Charity is dedicated to supporting independent musicians, small venues, and music education programs. Founded by rock legends, we understand the struggles of the music industry.

Every donation helps fund grants for artists, venue renovations, and youth music programs. Together, we're keeping the music alive.
